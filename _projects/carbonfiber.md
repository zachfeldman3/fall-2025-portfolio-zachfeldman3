#Introduction 
The goal of this proejct is when i first joined the team i realized that though the layups were producing pretty decnet results, a lot of the steps were necessary, tedious or could be improved. so i stepped in to do that. 

Once the mold we received from the CNC lab was assembled we would repair any crakcs or imperfectison with bondo (a surafce filler or whatever), then hand sand it up to 400 (out of fear of oversanding with a machine) and hten we'd spray with druatec, a surface primer so we can achieve a mirror finish prior to the layup. however, after doign some research i realized that people have had good results with spraying the duratec on a lower grit so i tested this by spraying a mold only sanded to 180 with druatec and actually found that we achieved better adhesion and a more uniform finish so we impoementd it into our process.. Depending on the size of the mold, this can save us up to 6 hours of hand sanding and sometimes even more.

After we spray the duratec is the longest part of the process. In the past we have wet hand sanded the duratec coated mold up to 2000
